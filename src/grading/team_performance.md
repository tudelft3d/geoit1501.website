
# Rubric for team performance

|Criteria	|Reflection questions|Exemplary|Proficient|	Developing|Needs Improvement|
|-|-|-|-|-|-|
|**Participation**|	Did everyone contribute? Who took initiative? Were responsibilities shared fairly?|	Consistently contributes meaningful ideas and effort.|	Participates regularly and completes assigned work.|	Participates inconsistently or needs prompting.|	Rarely contributes or does not complete responsibilities.|
|Communication|	Did members listen to each other? Were ideas expressed respectfully?	|Communicates clearly, listens actively, and encourages others.	|Usually communicates respectfully and listens to teammates.|Communication is sometimes unclear or interruptive.	|Poor communication limits group progress.|
|Collaboration|	How well did the group work together? Did members help one another?	|Builds on others' ideas, supports teammates, and works toward shared goals.	|Works well with others most of the time.	|Sometimes struggles to cooperate or compromise.|	Frequently works independently or creates conflict.|
|Responsibility & Accountability	Were deadlines met? Did everyone follow through on commitments?|	Completes tasks on time and helps ensure the group's success.|	Completes assigned tasks with minimal reminders.	|Occasionally misses deadlines or requires reminders.	|Frequently fails to complete assigned work.|
|Problem Solving|	How did the group respond to challenges? Were solutions collaborative?|	Identifies challenges and leads constructive solutions.	|Helps solve problems when they arise.	|Needs support to resolve issues.	|Avoids or worsens problems.|
|Respect & Inclusion|	Did everyone have opportunities to participate? Were different viewpoints valued?	|Values all perspectives and creates an inclusive environment.|	Treats others respectfully and includes most members.	|Sometimes overlooks or excludes others' ideas.|	Shows disrespect or excludes others.|
|Decision-Making	|How were decisions made? Did everyone have a voice?	|Decisions are made collaboratively using evidence and discussion.	|Most decisions involve group input.	|Some decisions are dominated by a few members.|	Decisions are made without meaningful collaboration.|
|Overall Effectiveness|	|What is the group's greatest strength? What should be improved?	|Group functioned exceptionally well and exceeded expectations.|	Group met objectives effectively.	|Group met some objectives but with noticeable difficulties.	|Group struggled to achieve objectives.|
